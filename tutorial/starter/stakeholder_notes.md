# SoopiFood Release 1: stakeholder notes

What the stakeholders told us during elicitation:

| Stakeholder | What they said |
|---|---|
| Founder | "The launch campaign is built around first-order promo codes. Without promo codes there is no launch campaign." |
| Operations | "About 70% of first-time food delivery customers in the city pay cash on delivery." |
| Marketing | "New users pick restaurants by star rating. Ratings and reviews are our main trust signal against the established apps." |
| Restaurants | "Customers often ask for changes (less spicy, no onion). Today this happens by phone, and orders go wrong." |
