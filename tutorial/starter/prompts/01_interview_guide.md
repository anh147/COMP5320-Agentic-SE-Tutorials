Context:
I'm a business analyst eliciting requirements for a new food delivery system.
- System: Customers order from local restaurants through a mobile app. Restaurants
  receive and manage orders on a tablet. Independent riders pick up and deliver.
- Stakeholder: A restaurant manager. They run day-to-day operations during service:
  accepting orders, coordinating the kitchen, handing food to riders, and handling
  complaints. They are not the owner and don't set pricing or sign contracts.
- Project stage: Early discovery. No design decisions have been made. The goal is
  to understand current workflows, pain points, and needs, not to validate features.

Task:
Create a semi-structured interview guide for a 45–60 minute interview with this manager.

Output format:
- Sections: Warm-up, Current workflow, Pain points, Coordination with riders and
  customers, Success and priorities, Wrap-up
- 8 to 10 open-ended questions total, ordered from warm-up to wrap-up
- 1 to 2 follow-up probes per question
- Avoid yes/no questions and questions that assume a specific feature or solution
- End with a short note on which questions to cut first if time runs short
