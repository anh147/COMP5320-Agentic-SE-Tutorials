Warm-up
1. Could you describe a typical shift for you, focusing on how you juggle your daily responsibilities?

What tasks consume the largest portion of your attention during peak dining hours?

How does managing delivery orders fit into your overall routine?

Current Workflow
2. Walk me through exactly what happens in your restaurant from the moment a delivery order comes in to the moment it leaves the kitchen.

How do you communicate the specific details and modifications to the kitchen staff?

How do you balance the priority between walk-in customers and incoming delivery tickets?

3. How do you track the progress of a delivery order once the kitchen starts preparing it?

What steps do you take if a dish is taking longer than expected to prepare?

How do you handle situations where an item runs out after an order has already been accepted?

Pain Points
4. Thinking about your busiest shifts, what are the most frustrating challenges your team faces with delivery operations?

Can you walk me through a recent instance where a delivery order went wrong and how it impacted the kitchen?

At what specific point in the order lifecycle do you feel you lose the most time or effort?

5. How do you currently handle situations where the restaurant is overwhelmed with orders?

What strategies do you use to regulate the flow of incoming delivery requests?

How does a rush impact your staff's ability to maintain accuracy?

Coordination with Riders and Customers
6. Tell me about the handoff process when a rider arrives to pick up the food.

What factors typically cause friction or bottlenecks between your staff and the riders?

How do you handle situations where a rider arrives too early or extremely late?

7. Walk me through your process for handling customer complaints related to a delivery order.

How do you typically investigate whether an issue (like a missing item or cold food) happened in the kitchen versus during transit?

What channels do you use to communicate with the customer to resolve the situation?

Success and Priorities
8. If a new operational system were introduced, what would a perfectly smooth Friday night look like for you and your staff?

What specific manual tasks would you want to spend less time worrying about?

What information is most critical for you to have at a glance to keep the kitchen running smoothly?

Wrap-up
9. Is there anything else about your experience managing delivery workflows that we haven't covered today but you feel is important for us to understand?

Who else on your team (e.g., a specific line cook or host) do you think would have valuable insights on this process?

Time Management Note:
If the interview runs short, cut Question 3 (tracking order progress) and Question 5 (handling volume overwhelm), as the natural flow of Questions 2 and 4 will likely reveal the most critical parts of these answers. Always retain the questions on pain points (Q4) and rider handoffs (Q6), as these are the most common operational bottlenecks for restaurant managers in delivery ecosystems.
